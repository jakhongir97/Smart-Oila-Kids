## English (U.S.)

### Subtitle
Child safety: location & SOS

### Promotional Text
Pair your child's iPhone with a 5-digit code from Oila360: location, SOS, chat and tasks. Live checks are shown on screen and nothing is ever recorded.

### Keywords
family,kids,location,gps,tracker,sos,chat,tasks,parent,safety,farzand,bolalar,ota-ona,oila

### Description
Bolajon360 is the child's half of the Oila360 family safety service. A parent installs it on their child's iPhone and links the two phones with a 5-digit code from the parent app. From then on the child's phone can share where it is, raise an alarm, and stay in touch with the family.

WHAT THE CHILD GETS
• A big SOS button. One tap, a confirmation, and the parent is alerted with the child's location and battery level. If there is no signal the alert is kept and retried for hours rather than lost.
• Chat with a parent — text and photos, with delivery and read marks.
• Tasks a parent sets, with stars for finishing them.
• A clear picture of what the app is doing: which permissions are on, whether the phone is reaching the family, and a screen-time figure for today.

WHAT THE PARENT SEES, IN THE OILA360 APP
• Where the phone is, and where it has been. Locations are sent in small batches, and only after the phone has actually moved.
• Battery level and whether the phone is on Wi-Fi or mobile data.
• SOS alerts the moment they are raised.

LIVE CHECKS, AND HOW THEY ARE SHOWN
A parent worried about a moment can ask to listen, or to see through the camera. This is deliberately not silent:
• The child agrees once, in the app, before the microphone or camera is ever opened. The camera needs its own separate agreement — saying yes to sound does not say yes to the camera.
• While a check is running, a red banner sits across the top of the app saying the parent is listening or watching, with a Stop button the child can press.
• If the app is not on screen, a notification says a live check is on and is re-posted while it lasts.
• Nothing is recorded. There is no recording feature in this app: the sound and picture are live only, and the session ends on its own after a short, server-set limit.
• Video only works while the app is open.

WHAT THIS APP DOES NOT DO
• It does not block or limit other apps, and it does not filter websites or content.
• It does not read messages, calls, browsing history or the photo library. Photos are only sent when the child picks one to send in chat.
• It does not record audio or video.
• It does not hide. The child can see it, open it, and see exactly what is on.

A parent can pause the app, which shows a full-screen "App is paused" notice — over Bolajon360 only, not the phone. SOS still works from that screen.

Bolajon360 needs the Oila360 parent app and an account to work. Location is used in the background so a parent can find the phone when it matters; you can turn it off at any time in iOS Settings.

Privacy policy: https://oila360.uz/en/privacy

### What's New in This Version
This version replaces the earlier Smart Oila Family Link release. The app is now Bolajon360, matching the Oila360 family service, and most of it has been rewritten.

New in this version
• Parent–child chat, with photos, delivery and read marks.
• Tasks and star rewards set by a parent.
• Live audio and video checks a parent can request — each one agreed to by the child first, shown by a red on-screen banner with a Stop button, announced by a notification when the app is not on screen, and never recorded.
• A pause screen a parent can put up, with SOS still reachable from it.

Better in this version
• Disconnecting the phone now really disconnects it. It tells the server, and the parent's app stops showing the phone as connected.
• If a parent has set a disconnect PIN, the phone asks for it and the server checks it.
• You are no longer asked for microphone or camera permission twice — answering during setup is enough.
• Location is sent in batches and only after real movement, which is easier on the battery.
• SOS alerts survive a bad connection instead of being dropped.
• The home screen handles long names properly, and the app is in Uzbek, Russian and English.

Родитель может попросить послушать или посмотреть через камеру. Это намеренно сделано не тайно:

• Ничего не записывается. В приложении нет записи: звук и изображение только в реальном времени, и сеанс сам заканчивается по короткому лимиту, заданному сервером.
• Видео работает только при открытом приложении.

ЧЕГО ЭТО ПРИЛОЖЕНИЕ НЕ ДЕЛАЕТ
• Не блокирует и не ограничивает другие приложения, не фильтрует сайты и контент.
• Не читает переписку, звонки, историю браузера и галерею. Фотография уходит только та, которую ребёнок сам выбрал в чате.
• Не записывает звук и видео.
• Не прячется. Ребёнок видит его, может открыть и посмотреть, что включено.

Родитель может поставить приложение на паузу — тогда появляется экран «Приложение приостановлено». Это касается только Bolajon360, а не всего телефона. SOS с этого экрана работает.

Для работы нужны родительское приложение Oila360 и аккаунт. Геолокация используется в фоне, чтобы родитель мог найти телефон, когда это важно; её можно отключить в настройках iOS в любой момент.

## BLOCKERS — do not press "Add for Review" until these are cleared

### 1. The published privacy policy describes a different app (HIGHEST STAKES)

`https://oila360.uz/{en,ru,uz}/privacy` is what the listing, and the app's own Settings row, send
App Review to. Fetched 2026-08-28 (HTTP 200, "Last updated: July 20, 2026"). It contradicts the
binary in three ways, and one of them is the dangerous direction:

- **"To function, the Bolajon360 app requests the following Android permissions."** The app being
  submitted is the iOS one. The policy never describes it.
- **"List of installed apps and their usage time (screen time statistics)"** is listed as collected.
  iOS cannot enumerate installed apps at all, the Screen Time engine is flag-off in this build, and
  nothing of the sort is sent.
- **"The RECORD_AUDIO and CAMERA permissions are not used in the current version and will be added
  in future updates."** Build 17 **ships live audio and video**. The policy tells parents the
  microphone and camera are not used, on an app installed on a child's phone, while the binary can
  open both.

That last point is under-disclosure of microphone and camera use for a minor's device. It is a
5.1.1(i) problem, it will not survive review alongside a description that leads on live checks, and
it is a real trust problem independent of Apple. **The policy has to be rewritten for iOS before
submission.** It is a website change, not an iOS one.

### 2. App Review cannot get into the app

The only way past the third screen is redeeming a live 5-digit pairing code, and codes expire in
about a minute. Every debug bypass is `#if DEBUG` and cannot exist in the archive — verified by
`strings` over the Release binary. Without a QA arrangement this is a 2.1 rejection with certainty.
Needed: a non-expiring, re-redeemable code bound to a demo child, plus a parent-side demo account
for `app.oila360.uz`. Do NOT solve it with a static code on the real pairing route.

### 3. Notes for Review must be written, and fit 4,000 characters

Not drafted here on purpose: every draft the review pass produced was rejected for shipping
unfilled placeholders (`[INSERT 5-DIGIT CODE]`), which is itself an automatic 2.1. Write it once the
QA code from (2) actually exists. It must cover: the pairing requirement and the live code;
justification for Always-location; the live-audio/video consent chain and the fact that nothing is
recorded; and why `UIBackgroundModes` includes `audio` (a parent-initiated check continues off
screen under a server-owned lease of at most 300 s, disclosed by a re-posted notification).

Two claims to avoid, because they are false and a reviewer can disprove them:
- that the live-session banner "cannot be covered" — it is a sibling row in RootView's stack, and a
  system alert or a sheet presented above the root will cover it;
- that a Focus mode causes an off-screen audio request to be refused — `presenceBannerWouldRenderNow`
  reads authorization, scheduled delivery, alert and lock-screen settings, not Focus.

### 4. Not verified on hardware

Build 17 has never run on a physical device. It is installed on an iPhone 15 Pro but the phone stayed
locked all session, and the iPhone 12 mini never came online.
